Notes
- Weather files contain each station's full history; processing filters to 2020-2024.
- GHCN-D values are in tenths (temperature in tenths of deg C, precipitation in tenths of mm) and are
  converted during processing.
- Products and stores registers are current snapshots (2026-10-01); historical product attributes come
  from the sales lines themselves (each sales row repeats item description, pack, volume, cost, retail).

KNOWN DATA ISSUES (to document in the data-quality report)
  - Census population estimates file is Latin-1 encoded (not UTF-8) - read with encoding='latin-1'.
  - 2021 has ~15% fewer invoice lines than other years (genuine, not a download issue).
  - Iowa Data Hub exports are split into multiple CSV parts inside each zip (part_0001 ...).
  - Products and stores are current snapshots; use attributes on each sales line for history.
